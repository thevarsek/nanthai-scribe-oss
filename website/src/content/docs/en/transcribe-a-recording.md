## What it does

> Warning: You must ask everyone for permission before you record or transcribe a conversation. Recording laws vary by country, and you are responsible for following them.

Any audio you already have, a voice memo, a recorded call, an interview, can be processed like a meeting: transcribed on the device, split into speakers if the speaker model is installed, and summarised with action items. The result appears under Meetings in the Library.

Supported formats: **m4a, mp3, wav, aac, caf, aiff**.

## On Mac

Either:

- Open the Library, click the arrow next to **Record** and choose **Transcribe a Recording…**, then pick the file; or
- drag an audio file from the Finder onto the Library window.

## On iPhone

1. In Voice Memos, Files or any app that shares audio, tap **Share**.
2. Choose **Scribe** in the share sheet.
3. Scribe opens and starts processing. Keep it open until the progress steps finish, or let it continue in the background; if iOS pauses the work, it resumes the next time you open Scribe.

You can also use **Transcribe a Recording…** from the Record menu in the Library and pick a file from Files.

## What you'll see

- The meeting's **title** is the file name without its extension and its **date** is the file's creation date. Both can be changed.
- The same processing steps as a live meeting: Saving audio, Transcribing, Identifying speakers (if the model is installed), Summarising.
- The file is **copied** into Scribe's meeting storage; your original is not touched. As with a live meeting, the copy is deleted once the transcript exists unless **Keep recordings' audio** is on, in which case you can [play it back](/docs/meeting-playback).

## Common problems

**"The meeting language isn't installed."** Transcription uses your dictation languages. Add the language of the recording under Settings › Languages first, and download the language pack if it needs one.

**The speakers are all "Speaker 1".** Install the 13.7 MB speaker model (Mac: Settings › Meetings; both: the toggle on the recording sheet) and choose **Retry transcription** from the meeting's menu.

**A long file stops early on iPhone.** Processing is capped at four hours of audio, the same as a live recording.
