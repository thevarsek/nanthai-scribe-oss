## Talk or letters

The Scribe keyboard opens on the **Talk** layer. Tap **ABC** for the letter keyboard; the **Scribe** key brings you back. If you type more than you dictate, set **Settings › Keyboard › Opens with** to **Letters** and Scribe becomes your everyday keyboard with a Talk key one tap away.

The letter layer is laid out like Apple's: the same rows and key sizes, Shift and caps lock, **123** for numbers and **#+=** for symbols, a globe key when iOS needs one, and Return.

## Suggestions and autocorrect

The suggestion bar shows three candidates drawn from the system dictionary, your [Dictionary](/docs/dictionary), remembered keyboard words and your text replacements. When the middle suggestion is about to replace what you typed, the left slot shows your word in quotes; tap it to keep your spelling.

Autocorrect follows your enabled languages and applies when you type a space or punctuation. Tap **Delete** straight after a correction to put your word back. Correct the same word twice and the keyboard remembers it; see [Auto-learning](/docs/auto-learning#keyboard-words).

Autocorrect and suggestions work in 33 languages: Afrikaans, Albanian, Basque, Bosnian, Catalan, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, Galician, German, Hungarian, Icelandic, Indonesian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Malay, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Portuguese (Brazil), Romanian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish, Tagalog, Turkish and Vietnamese.

## Quotes and punctuation

Apostrophes and quotation marks come out curly, in the style of the language you are typing: “ ” in English, „ “ in German, « » with narrow spaces in French. Two hyphens become a dash; a double space ends the sentence with a full stop. The Code Refine tone keeps straight quotes in dictated code; it does not change what you type.

## Accents and alternates

Long-press a letter for its accents and alternates, chosen for your enabled languages. Long-press a top-row letter to type the digit shown on it when hints are on.

## Numbers

**Settings › Keyboard › Numbers** has three settings:

- **Off**: numbers live on the 123 layer.
- **Hints**: small digits appear on the top row of letters; long-press to type one.
- **Row**: a full row of number keys above the letters.

## Taps that learn your aim

Where a tap lands is scored against the nearest keys and the words of your language, so an off-centre tap still produces the word you meant. Over time the keyboard learns where you actually tap each key and shifts its centre, by at most a quarter of the key, to match.

Choose **Settings › Keyboard › Reset typing learning** to clear the learned offsets and the remembered keyboard words and start from the default layout.

## Delete and the space bar

- Hold **Delete** to repeat; keep holding to delete words. Swipe left from Delete to delete whole words; the keyboard says how many.
- Long-press Delete and tap **Undo** to put back what you just removed.
- Press and hold the **space bar** to move the cursor like a trackpad.

## Settings › Keyboard

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Opens with | Talk or Letters as the layer shown first. |
| Suggestions | The suggestion bar. Refine presets still appear when text is selected. |
| Numbers | Off, Hints or Row. |
| Swipe to type | Turn [swipe typing](/docs/swipe-typing) on or off. |
| Keep mic ready for | Off, 1, 5 or 15 minutes after a dictation. |
| Lower other audio while I talk | Ducks music and video while you dictate. |
| Reset typing learning | Clears learned key positions and keyboard words. |

Full Access lets the keyboard exchange text and settings with the Scribe app on the same phone. It makes no network requests.

## Common problems

**Letters are missing when I type fast.** Tell us the iPhone model at support@nanthai.tech. The typing layer has been tuned against the system keyboard; this should not happen.

**A word keeps being corrected to something else.** Tap the left suggestion to keep your spelling; after two keeps the keyboard stops correcting it. Or add the word to your Dictionary.

**Chinese, Japanese or Korean.** The Scribe keyboard has no input method for these scripts. Use Apple's keyboards for typing them, via the globe key. Dictation depends on what Apple's speech offers on your device.
