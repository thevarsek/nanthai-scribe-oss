## Keep the audio

By default Scribe deletes a meeting's audio once the transcript exists; the transcript is what you keep. To be able to listen again, turn on **Keep recordings' audio** in **Settings › Meetings** before you record. The same applies to recordings you import with [Transcribe a recording](/docs/transcribe-a-recording): the copy Scribe makes is kept only if the setting is on.

## Play

Open a meeting that has audio. A player bar sits above the transcript with:

- **Play / Pause**;
- a **scrubber** showing the position and the total length;
- a **speed** button cycling **1×**, **1.5×** and **2×**.

Tap or click any **timestamp** in the transcript to jump to that moment. Playback follows the transcript, so you can check what was actually said while you edit a turn.

## Remove the audio

Turn off **Keep recordings' audio** to stop keeping audio for future meetings; existing recordings are not touched. Delete a meeting to remove its audio with it; it waits 30 days in [Recently Deleted](/docs/recently-deleted), then the space is freed.

## Common problems

**There is no player.** The audio was not kept for this meeting. The setting applies to meetings recorded after you turn it on.
