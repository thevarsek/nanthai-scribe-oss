## Choose your shortcut

1. Open **Settings › General**. The **Talk shortcut** row shows the current keys.
2. Click **Change…**. A small panel says **Press a key or combination**.
3. Press the shortcut you want. The panel closes and the row updates. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.

Or pick one of the presets from the menu next to it: Right <kbd>⌥</kbd> (the default), Right <kbd>⌘</kbd>, <kbd>fn</kbd>, <kbd>⌃⌥Space</kbd> or <kbd>F13</kbd>.

## What works

- **A single modifier**: right <kbd>⌥</kbd>, right <kbd>⌘</kbd>, right <kbd>⌃</kbd> or <kbd>fn</kbd>. The left-hand keys are left alone, so normal shortcuts keep working.
- **A combination** with <kbd>⌃</kbd>, <kbd>⌥</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd>, such as <kbd>⌃⌥Space</kbd>.
- **A function key** on its own, <kbd>F1</kbd> to <kbd>F20</kbd>.

A plain letter or digit is refused ("Add ⌃, ⌥ or ⌘"), and so are shortcuts macOS uses itself ("That shortcut is used by macOS.").

## Foot pedals and buttons

Set your pedal, Stream Deck key or other button to send a key between <kbd>F13</kbd> and <kbd>F20</kbd>, then press it while the **Change…** panel is open. Hold the pedal to talk, release to insert. Everything else works the same: tap <kbd>Shift</kbd> for hands-free, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> for Refine.

## During a dictation

Press these **after** you have started holding the shortcut:

- <kbd>Shift</kbd> switches to hands-free. <kbd>Return</kbd> finishes; <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> turns Refine on.
- With text selected, <kbd>⌘</kbd> makes the dictation an instruction for that selection.

## Permissions

**Accessibility** lets Scribe notice your shortcut, paste the text, read a selection and, optionally, use screen context. Grant it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. The shortcut works as soon as access is granted; no relaunch is needed.

## Common problems

**A single <kbd>fn</kbd> opens Emoji or Dictation instead.** Change what fn does in System Settings › Keyboard › "Press 🌐 key to", or choose another shortcut.

**My combination triggers something else in one app.** Apps see the combination too. Choose one no app uses, or a function key.
