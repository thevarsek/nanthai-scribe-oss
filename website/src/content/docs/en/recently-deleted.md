## Where deleted items go

Deleting a note or a meeting moves it to **Recently Deleted**, a section at the bottom of the Library. Items stay there for **30 days**, then Scribe removes them the next time it launches. Deleting is still one swipe or one click; there is no confirmation to get through.

## Restore an item

1. Open **Recently Deleted** at the bottom of the Library.
2. Find the note or meeting.
3. Choose **Restore**. The item goes back to where it was, with its pin, edits and original text intact.

## Delete for good

- **Delete Now** on one item removes it immediately.
- **Delete All** in the section's menu empties Recently Deleted.

Either frees the space at once, including a meeting's kept audio. There is no way back after this.

## What is not kept here

Untouched dictations that expire under your **Keep history** period are removed directly; they are not moved to Recently Deleted. Pin or edit a dictation to keep it.

> Tip: A backup made with **Export Library…** includes only items that are not deleted. Export before you empty Recently Deleted if you are unsure.
