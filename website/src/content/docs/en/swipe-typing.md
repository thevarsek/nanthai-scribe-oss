## Swipe a word

1. On the letter keyboard, put your finger on the first letter.
2. Slide through the other letters without lifting. A trail follows your finger.
3. Lift. The word is inserted with a space after it.

The suggestion bar shows the swiped word in the middle and two alternatives either side; tap one to replace the word. Typing punctuation straight after a swipe removes the extra space first.

## Undo a swipe

Tap **Delete** straight after a swipe to remove the whole word, not just the last letter.

## Languages

Swipe uses a bundled word list for each of your enabled languages, plus your own Dictionary words. Word lists are bundled for all 34 autocorrect languages (see [The keyboard](/docs/keyboard#suggestions-and-autocorrect)). Languages without a word list still work by tapping.

## Turn it off

**Settings › Keyboard › Swipe to type**. With it off, sliding across the keys does nothing and taps work as before.

## Common problems

**The wrong word keeps coming out.** Lift between words, and start and end on the right letters; the middle of the path matters less. Add names to your [Dictionary](/docs/dictionary) so they can be swiped.

**No trail appears.** Swipe to type is off, or the keyboard is on a layer without letters.
