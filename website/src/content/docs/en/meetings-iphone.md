## Record an in-person meeting

> Warning: You must ask everyone for permission before you record or transcribe a conversation. Recording laws vary by country, and you are responsible for following them.

1. Open the Library on the **Dictate** tab and tap **Record** in the toolbar.
2. The recording sheet says **Ask everyone for permission before you record. Recording laws vary by country.** It also offers **Tell speakers apart**, a one-time 13.7 MB model download.
3. Tap **Start recording**.

**iPhone records the microphone only.** iOS does not let an app capture other apps' call audio. To record both sides of an online call, use [Meetings on Mac](/docs/meetings).

Recording needs at least 1 GB free; otherwise Scribe says **Not enough space to record. Free up 1 GB and try again.**

## While recording

A full-screen view shows the elapsed time, the microphone level, **Pause** and **Stop**. Tap **Minimise** to keep recording while you use the phone; the Library shows a **Recording · 12:04** banner you can tap to come back. Recording continues with the screen locked or Scribe in the background.

The Lock Screen Live Activity has **Pause**, **Resume** and **Stop**, and a coral dot in the Dynamic Island. Phone calls and Siri take the microphone; Scribe pauses and resumes when iOS lets it. If it can't resume on its own, a notification says **Meeting paused. Tap to resume.**

## Limits

Meetings stop and process at **four hours**; a **Stops in 10 min** notice appears before that. Recording also stops, and processes what it has, when the battery drops to 5 % or free space falls under 100 MB.

## Finish and process

Tap **Stop**. Scribe saves the audio, transcribes it, identifies speakers when the model is installed, and writes a summary with action items. Processing can continue in the background with system progress; if iOS pauses the work, the meeting shows **Paused processing** and resumes the next time you open Scribe.

The summary follows your Refine engine setting. Gemma 4 runs while the app is in the foreground; background summarisation uses Apple Intelligence.

## Read, edit and share

Open the meeting in the Library. Read the summary, add notes, and tap a transcript turn to edit it. Tap a speaker name to rename it. Timestamps on speaker-labelled turns jump to that point in the audio when it was kept; see [Meeting audio playback](/docs/meeting-playback).

**Copy** offers Summary, Transcript or Everything; **Share** exports Markdown or plain text. **Update summary** regenerates the summary after edits; **Retry transcription** reprocesses the audio.

## Settings › Meetings

- **Show reminder before recording**.
- **Keep recordings' audio**: off by default. Audio is removed after processing unless this is on.
- **Speaker detection · 13.7 MB**: download or delete the speaker model.

Everything is processed on the iPhone. No recording, transcript or summary is uploaded.

## Common problems

**The recording stopped by itself.** Check the time limit and the battery and storage limits above. What was recorded is processed normally.

**"The microphone stopped delivering audio."** Another app or a call took the microphone and it did not come back. Reopen the recording and tap Resume, or Stop to process what you have.

**Everyone is "Speaker 1".** Install the speaker model under Settings › Meetings, then choose **Retry transcription**.
